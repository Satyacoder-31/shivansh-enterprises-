We provide full-spectrum engineering services—from initial site survey and precision optical calibration to rooftop solar structural mounting and long-term technical maintenance in Keshod and across Gujarat.

SERVICE 01

CCTV Installation & Site Survey

Our certified technicians perform comprehensive on-site perimeter inspections to identify critical blind spots, entry vectors, and lighting transitions. We handle complete conduit casing, weatherproof cable termination, and precision mounting.

  • Residential perimeter walls, main gates, and terrace coverage
  • Commercial retail cash counters, warehouses, and loading bays
  • Clean, architectural concealed cabling with zero visual clutter
SERVICE 02

CCTV Configuration & Network Setup

We configure digital video recorders (DVR), network video recorders (NVR), and cloud telemetry apps. Whether you have local broadband or rely strictly on 4G SIM cameras, we guarantee seamless remote access from your smartphone.

  • Coreprix Pro, HF AIoT, and CP PLUS mobile application setup
  • Smart push notifications for AI human detection and motion trips
  • Encrypted multi-user streaming for families and business managers

SERVICE 03

Solar Feasibility & Consultation

Before installing solar panels, structural geometry and azimuth orientation must be calculated. We evaluate roof shadow trajectories, concrete slab strength, and monthly electricity billing data to recommend the exact kW capacity required.

  • Accurate generation modeling without misleading savings promises
  • Structural engineering inspection for high-wind stability
  • Liaison guidance for utility bi-directional net-metering approvals
SERVICE 04

LED Solution Guidance & Lux Planning

Lighting is an architectural discipline. We assist architects, interior decorators, and homeowners in choosing the ideal color temperatures (2700K warm, 3000K soft white, 4000K natural), beam angles, and anti-glare downlight spacing.

  • Lux calculation for retail jewelry showrooms and boutiques
  • Continuous linear LED profiles for false ceiling coves and staircases
  • High-durability commercial warehouse and facade floodlighting
